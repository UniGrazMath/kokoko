presenter: Isaac Owino Okoth

title: Higher dimensional plane trees with marked edges
---

In this talk, we introduce the concept of marked $d$-dimensional plane trees and present
their enumeration according to four key statistics: the number of vertices, the number
of marked edges, the degree of the root, and the number of leaves. Using the symbolic
method, we establish generating functions that encode these parameters and employ the
Lagrange inversion formula to derive explicit closed-form enumeration formulas. Furthermore,
we establish explicit bijections between marked $d$-dimensional plane trees and three
distinct classes of lattice paths: Dyck paths with labelled up-steps and coloured down-steps,
labelled skew Dyck paths, and coloured $d$-Dyck paths. These bijections not only
establish structural correspondences between the combinatorial objects but also provide
combinatorial interpretations of the derived generating functions and enumeration formulas.
