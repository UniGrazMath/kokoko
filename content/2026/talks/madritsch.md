title: Limit theorems for the length of partitions in small powers
---

The study of the partition function $p(n)$, counting the number of solutions of the
equation $n = a_1 + \cdots + a_\ell$ over integers $1 \le a_1 \le \cdots \le a_\ell$, has
a long history in combinatorics. In the present talk we consider the following variant
of this question: partitions in integers of the form

$$
n = \lfloor a_1^\alpha \rfloor + \cdots + \lfloor a_\ell^\alpha \rfloor
$$

with $1 \le a_1 < \cdots < a_\ell$ and $0 < \alpha < 1$ a fixed real. Using the saddle
point method we show a central and a local limit theorem for the number of summands in a
random partition of that kind.

This is joint work with Gabriel Lipnik and Robert Tichy.
