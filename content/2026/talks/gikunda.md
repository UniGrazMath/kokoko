presenter: Dennis Kinoti Gikunda

title: On Log-Normality of the Norm in Restricted Integer Partitions
---

The norm of a partition of $n$ is defined as the product of its parts. The distribution
of the norm in partitions of $n$ was recently studied by Bridges and Craig, who showed,
using the method of moments, that this parameter does not converge to a non-trivial
distribution as $n \to \infty$. In this talk, I will present joint work with Dimbinaina
Ralaivaosaona (Chapter 4 of my PhD thesis, *The Distribution of the Product of Parts in
Integer Partitions*), in which we consider restricted partitions, where parts are not
allowed to repeat. We prove that, under a general framework due to Meinardus, the norm
is asymptotically log-normally distributed in this setting. Our approach is inspired by
a Vershik-type weight function, which allows us to treat a broad class of restricted
partition models within a single argument. Examples include standard partitions,
partitions into powers, and prime partitions; the latter case is of particular interest
in number theory.
