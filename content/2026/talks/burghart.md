title: On cyclic points in multiset permutations
---

Let $f \colon [n] \to [n]$ be a mapping; its cyclic points are those $i \in [n]$ for which
$f^{\circ k}(i) = i$ for some $k \ge 1$. Using a bijection between cyclic points and so-called
terminal closers we prove distributional limit laws for cyclic points of multiset
permutations under general conditions. Extending this result to the class of all mappings
re-establishes classical results by Stepanov, Katz, and Rényi; extending it instead to the
class of parking functions turns out to yield identical limit laws. This confirms the
“equivalence of ensembles” between mappings and parking functions which was first observed
by Diaconis and Hicks for other statistics. Based on joint work with Calum Buchanan,
Stephan Wagner, and Mei Yin.
