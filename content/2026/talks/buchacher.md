presenter: Manfred Buchacher
affiliation: TU Graz
title: A differential analogue of Tutte’s invariant method
---

The research this talk is about is part of the collective effort of enumerating restricted lattice
walks by solving the functional equations their generating functions satisfy. I will talk
about one of the many methods these equations can be approached with: Tutte’s invariant
method. I will recall the method, discuss what it relies on, and present a differential
analogue thereof. The talk is based on <https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10377> and joint
and ongoing work with Charlotte Hardouin.
