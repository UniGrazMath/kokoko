title: Constructions of caps
---

A cap in an affine or projective space is a point set without any three points on any line.
We work in the affine space ($\mathbb{F}_p^n$) and give improved constructions.

Such constructions are closely related with progression-free sets in $\mathbb{F}_p^n$ or in
the integers and we sketch the most recent improvements.
