title: Distinct flattened partitions avoiding a pattern of length four
---

Let $P_n$ denote the set of distinct permutations of length $n$ that arise from the
flattening process applied to the partitions of $[n] = \{1, \ldots, n\}$. The problem of
avoidance of a single classical pattern of length four by members of $P_n$ is considered.
Let $p_n(\tau)$ denote the number of members of $P_n$ that avoid the pattern $\tau$.
We show that $p_n(\tau) = C_{n-1}$ for all $n \ge 1$ for seven patterns of length four
yielding new combinatorial interpretations of the Catalan number sequence. Further, we
show that $p_n(\tau)$ corresponds to the binomial transform of Catalan numbers for three
other patterns. To establish our results, we refine the counting sequence $p_n(\tau)$ in
each case so as to obtain a system of functional equations satisfied by the corresponding
generating functions. These functional equations are then solved explicitly leading to a
determination of $p_n(\tau)$ in each case. Joint work with Toufik Mansour and Mark Shattuck.
