presenter: Pierre Bonnet

title: An Algebraicity Strategy for Quadrant Walks
---

The enumeration of walks in the quarter plane confined in the first quadrant has attracted
a lot of attention over the past twenty years. The generating functions associated to small
steps models satisfy a functional equation in two catalytic variables. For such models,
Bousquet-Mélou and Mishna, along with Fayolle, Iasnogorodski and Malyshev, defined a group
called the group of the walk which turned out to be central in the classification of small steps
models. In particular, its action on the catalytic variables yields a set of changes of variables
compatible with the structure of the functional equation. This particular set called the
orbit has been generalized to models with arbitrary large steps by Bostan, Bousquet-Mélou
and Melczer, with no underlying group. In this talk, I will explain how one can endow the
orbit with the action of a Galois group, which extends the group of the walk to models
with large steps. The talk will then focus on the first major application of this Galoisian
framework to the study of walks, that is a generic algebraicity strategy for walks with
small backwards steps. This strategy is based on Tutte’s invariants method, introduced by
Bernardi, Bousquet-Mélou and Raschel. Namely, the Galois-theoretic formulation allows
for a complete characterization and construction of rational invariants and decouplings
that are crucial for this strategy. In the end, this allows us to prove the algebraicity of many
models with large steps. Talk based (in part) on a joint work with Charlotte Hardouin:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11084>.
